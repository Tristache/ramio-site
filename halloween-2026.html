Halloween event · October 22 to November 4, 2026

The Haunted Table

For two weeks, the table of Ramio, our Manipulation Rummy game, turns into a haunted one. A ten-box calendar builds it piece by piece: one box per day you open the app, and a missed day loses nothing.

The event arrives in a mid-October update. Nothing to buy.

How it works

One box a day, a table built piece by piece

One box a day

Open the app on any day from October 22 to November 4 and the next box opens. Ten boxes in fourteen days: a missed day loses nothing.

Four pieces build the table

Boxes 1 to 4 are puzzle pieces: the table and its pumpkin, the music, the magic touches, the night round. Each piece brings its part of the table to life.

Six keepsakes

Boxes 5 to 10 hold three card backs and three characters. They are kept for good and can be used on any table.

The calendar

Complete the puzzle, keep the table until November 30

With all four pieces, the haunted table stays until November 30. Then it leaves for every player, until next year's edition.

A real game, haunted

Melds on the felt, a hand to play, two opponents wearing Vlad and Morgana: this is an actual game running on the Halloween table. The candle in the pumpkin flickers, and witches on broomsticks circle it, jumping over its stem.
